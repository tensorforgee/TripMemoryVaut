## App name

Trip Memory Vault

## Short description

Keep travel photos, places and memories in a local archive you control.

## Full description

Revisit your journeys through photos, places and the details you remember.
Trip Memory Vault keeps a travel archive on your Android device, with no app
account required.

Create trips with exact, approximate or unknown dates. Import selected photos,
write memories, arrange stops and stays, and organize trips with companions and
life chapters. Keep dream places alongside the places you have visited, and
search your archive on your device.

Explore your trip on a map and add photo memory pins. Remembered connections are
approximate. Optional road previews use openrouteservice with your own API key
and your confirmation to send route coordinates. They are temporary suggestions,
not saved historical routes.

Your saved local content remains available offline. Detailed online basemaps
require internet access; a coarse local overview provides a fallback. Online map
requests reveal the viewed map area to the map provider.

Export a portable copy with original media, structured data and a readable
journal. Restore a supported archive into an empty vault. Exports can contain
location metadata and personal memories and are not encrypted by the app.

Android 9 or later is recommended and required for photo import and preview
regeneration in this build. Although Android 7–8.1 installation is allowed,
those photo functions are unavailable and older versions are unverified.

There is no automatic cloud backup or sync. Keep a verified export outside your
phone before uninstalling or replacing your device.

## Internal release notes

Initial internal test of the local trip archive: selected-photo import, memories,
stops, photo map pins, local search, and portable export/restore. Please use
Android 9+ and synthetic test content. Optional road previews require your own
ORS key and are discarded when the map session ends. Report failures and device
details through the tester feedback channel.
